Source: https://niharullal.github.io/nyc-dynasty-site (repo `niharullal/nyc-dynasty-site`, commit `82525b5`, copied 2026-11-13). Kept by Nihar; content generated by the commissioner.

## What is stored here
- `site/library/newsletters/week-01 … week-08/` — weekly preview pages for Weeks 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 (46 pages, JPEG). Week 3 was an auto-generated page at the source and Week 9 is not in the source archive.
- `site/library/history/` — the five-page League History series "Draft vs. Trade" (2022 startup through 2026 Week 7).
- `site/data/library.json` — manifest: title, local file, thumbnail and the original URL of every page. The Archives page ("Newsletter library") and weekly recaps read it.
- The Commissioner's Playbook 2026-27 PDF is linked, not copied (it contains dues amounts and league in-jokes; copy it only if the commissioner asks).
- Player photographs in the matchup pages were blanked in the local copies; each page links to its original. Do not restore them.

To refresh: clone the source repo, re-run the masking and copy steps (see git history for this commit), and rebuild `library.json`.

## Rules taken from the Playbook (now applied on the site)
- Six playoff teams, **reseeded every round**, high seed plays low seed.
- Rookie draft: non-playoff teams pick 1–6 in reverse order of regular-season finish; playoff teams pick 7–12 in reverse order of playoff finish; the 6 seed can never receive a top-six pick.
- Week 12 trade deadline, 4 taxi slots, 1 IR spot.
- Note: Sleeper's own bracket setting is still "fixed". The site follows the Playbook.

## House style of the weekly previews (for tuning `scripts/build-weekly.mjs`)
- **Overview page:** a short banner in the form "7-0 ALAN. 6-1 NIHAR. NEXT UP." or "WEEK 5 RAISES THE STAKES." with one line under it; six matchup rows with projected points and win chance; NFL slate with byes; "Commentary on the week" (one paragraph on last week); "Next league event" card (date, place, games on TV, rivalry of the week).
- **Matchup headline:** always two short capitalised sentences. Line one is the history or stakes hook, line two is this week's twist. Examples of the pattern: series record then projected edge; a streak then who needs it to end; a named NFL game that several starters share ("FIVE STARTERS SHARE PANTHERS-BUCCANEERS").
- **Tags:** RAZOR-THIN, FAIR FIGHT, UPSET WATCH, BLOWOUT POTENTIAL, PERSONAL RIVALRY / PLAYBOOK RIVALRY, FIRST MANAGER-TO-MANAGER MEETING, LINEUP WATCH. The site now uses these labels (razor-thin under 57%; upset watch when the favorite trails the series; fair fight under 75%; blowout potential above), and headlines follow the two-line pattern (`one` and `two` in `scripts/build-weekly.mjs`).
- **Sections in order:** headline, featured players, score line with win chance, one-paragraph story, When it swings (by kickoff window: Thu, international, Sun early, Sun PM, Sun night, Mon), Head-to-head history with regular/playoff/Toilet Bowl split, How X wins (three bullets each, facts only), Starting lineups.
- **Specials:** one-off analysis pages ("The Schedule Luck Report", "Behind the Records": all-play record, expected wins, matchup effect per manager).
- **Tone:** dry, factual, numbers in every sentence, no advice.
